## Current work

I am currently working on nonlocal quantum phase transitions, squeezing in standard and two-photon Tavis–Cummings models, and pattern formation in Rydberg exciton-polariton condensates. My work combines analytical modeling with numerical simulations in Python, Julia, Mathematica, and HPC environments.
